# Product Film

Start with `npm run plan -- "<brief>"` and inherit all delivery/runtime/QA constraints.

Prioritize:
- `scene.hero-product`
- `reveal.hero-settle`
- `camera.macro-push`
- `camera.parallax-2_5d`
- `transition.depth-cut` / `match-move`
- `audio.impact-stack`
- `camera.product-orbit` only when its runtime status is satisfied.

Research normally comes from the routed subset of `video-shotcraft`, `onda`, `motion-design-skill`, `product-launch-video-skill`, `remotion-skills`, and spatial donors only when needed.

Product films need contrast between movement and rest. Do not turn every feature into an equal-length animated card. Reserve large camera/impact moments for narrative pivots and payoff.
