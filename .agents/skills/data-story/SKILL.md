# Data Story

Run `npm run plan -- "<brief>"`; treat data values, axes, ordering, units and annotations as product truth.

Primary primitives:
- `data.chart-build`
- `data.metric-proof`
- `typography.metric-counter`
- restrained typography/reveal primitives for annotation.

Research normally routes to `chuk-motion`, `motion-canvas-examples`, `remotion-skills`, and `motion-skills`.

Rules:
- animate analytical causality, not every available mark;
- preserve scales/values throughout transitions;
- avoid simultaneous-series reveals unless comparison requires it;
- use camera effects sparingly around data;
- sample QA frames must prove the chart remains truthful at intermediate animation states.
